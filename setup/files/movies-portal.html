🖼️ Posters

🖼️
Checking…

Optional. Plex and Jellyfin give their own posters; these fill the gaps: Netflix titles, and titles a server has no poster for. Both keys are free: TMDB's from themoviedb.org (your account's Settings, API), OMDb's from omdbapi.com. TMDB is tried first. Keys stay on the box. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

📺 TV

📺
Checking…

Pairing

The TV must be on and on the home Wi-Fi. Choosing an LG or a Samsung shows a prompt on the TV: press Accept (or Allow) there within a minute. A Fire TV needs ADB debugging on (Settings, My Fire TV, Developer options) and asks to allow the box. Roku and LG are tested; the other kinds are built but haven't been tried on a real TV yet.

🩺 How it's doing

Checking…

It checks by itself every time it starts. Updating can take a few minutes; the tablet keeps working meanwhile.

⏻ Power

Restart fixes most problems and takes about a minute. After a shut down, it stays off until someone presses its power button.

Optional: a fresh start each night keeps it running smoothly. If someone is using the tablet then (or a video is playing from it), it waits until they stop, for up to 3 hours.
